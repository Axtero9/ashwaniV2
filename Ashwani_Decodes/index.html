SUNDAY DECODES #42

The 20-Minute Dopamine Reset

Written by Ashwani · 2 min read

When you feel restless, distracted, and unable to focus on deep work, your baseline dopamine isn't depleted—it's scattered across micro-rewards.

Every time you check a push notification, reply to a reel, or glance at stocks, you trigger a tiny phasic spike followed by a dip beneath baseline. Do this 30 times an hour, and your tonic dopamine flatlines. The result? Sluggishness and mental fatigue.

The Sunday Protocol:
  • • Step 1: Place phone in a closed drawer for 20 continuous minutes.
  • • Step 2: Do a zero-dopamine activity: look out a window, brew hot tea, or stretch.
  • • Step 3: Notice the urge to check. Do not fight it; watch the craving wave peak and subside.

"Friction is the ultimate antidote to habit loops. Enjoy your Sunday."
